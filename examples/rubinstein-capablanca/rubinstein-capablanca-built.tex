\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Nf6}{6...\figN f6} is the normal move in this variation. White's development was first introduced by
Schlechter and elaborated later on by Rubinstein. It aims at the isolation of Black's d-pawn,
against which the White pieces are gradually concentrated. In making the text move I was trying to
avoid the beaten track. Being a developing move there should be no objection to it in the way of
general principles, except that the Knights ought to come out before the Bishops.

\Move{\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2}{7.\figB g2} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7}{\figB e7} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O}{8.0--0} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8}{\figR c8}}\label{rc8}

In pursuance of the idea of changing the normal course of this variation, but with very poor
success. The move in theory ought to be unsound, since Black's king's knight is yet undeveloped. I
had not yet learned of the attack founded on \figN g5 and the exchange of the Bishop on e6. Either
\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Nf6}{8...\figN f6} or \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_h6}{8...h6}, to prevent either \figB{} or \figN g5, was right.

\Move{\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5}{9.dxc5} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5}{\figB xc5} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5}{10.\figN g5} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6}{\figN f6} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6}{11.\figN xe6} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6}{fxe6} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3}{12.\figB h3} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7}{\figQ e7} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5}{13.\figB g5} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5_O-O}{0--0}}

This is a mistake. The right move was \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5_Rd8}{13...\figR d8} in order to get the Rook away from the line
of the Bishop on h3 and at the same time to support the d-pawn. Incidentally it shows that White
failed to take proper advantage of Black's weak opening moves. Against the text move White makes a
very fine combination which I had seen, but which I thought could be defeated.

\Move{\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5_O-O_Bxf6}{14.\figB xf6} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5_O-O_Bxf6_Qxf6}{\figQ xf6}}\label{bxf6}

I considered \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/d4_d5_Nf3_c5_c4_e6_cxd5_exd5_Nc3_Nc6_g3_Be6_Bg2_Be7_O-O_Rc8_dxc5_Bxc5_Ng5_Nf6_Nxe6_fxe6_Bh3_Qe7_Bg5_O-O_Bxf6_gxf6}{14...gxf6}, which it seemed would give me a playable game, but I thought White's
