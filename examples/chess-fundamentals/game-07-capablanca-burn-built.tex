In this variation there is the alternative of developing this Bishop via g7, after \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_g6}{6...g6}.

Evidently to make room for the Queen at e7, but I do not think the move advisable at this stage. \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Be6}{11...\figB e6} is a more natural and effective move. It develops a piece and threatens \figB c4, which would have to be stopped.

\Move{\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5}{12.\figB g5} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6}{\figB e6}}

Now it is not so effective, because White's Queen's Bishop is out, and the Knight, in going to e3 to defend the square c4, does not block the Queen's Bishop.

\Move{\href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6_Ne3}{13.\figN e3} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6_Ne3_Re8}{\figR e8} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6_Ne3_Re8_O-O}{14.0--0} \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6_Ne3_Re8_O-O_Qe7}{\figQ e7}}

This is bad. Black's game was already not good. He probably had no choice but to take the Knight with the Bishop before making this move.

in order to bring it to d7, to support the other Knight and also his King's Pawn. White, however, does not allow time for this, and by taking advantage of his superior position is able to win a Pawn.

Since he had no way to prevent the loss of a Pawn, he should have given it up where it is, and played \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e5_Nf3_Nc6_Bb5_a6_Ba4_Nf6_d3_d6_c3_Be7_Nbd2_O-O_Nf1_b5_Bc2_d5_Qe2_dxe4_dxe4_Bc5_Bg5_Be6_Ne3_Re8_O-O_Qe7_Nd5_Bxd5_exd5_Nb8_a4_Nbd7}{17...\figN bd7}, in order to make his position more solid. The text move not only loses a Pawn, but leaves Black's game very much weakened.

With a Pawn more and all his pieces ready for action, while Black is still backward in development, it only remains for White to drive home his advantage before Black can come out with his pieces, in which case, by using the open h-file, Black might be able to start a strong attack against White's King. White is able by his next move to eliminate all danger.
