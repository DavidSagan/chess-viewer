He should have played \href{https://lichess.org/analysis/pgn/e4_e6_d4_d5_Nc3_Nf6_Bd3_dxe4_Nxe4_Nbd7_Nxf6_Nxf6_Nf3_Be7_Qe2_O-O_Bg5_h6_Bxf6_Bxf6_Qe4_g6_h4_Qd5}{12...\figQ d5} and tried to fight the game out that way.
